Retro Gaming Hub

Legal classic re-releases, community enhancement patches & sandboxed local runner

Getting Started

The Retro Hub is a 100% legal preservation companion — no copyrighted ROMs, BIOS files or other protected assets are ever hosted, indexed or uploaded.

  1. Browse the Legal Directory for official re-releases, retro compilations and subscription catalogs (NSO, PS Plus Classics, Antstream).
  2. Drop a legally dumped ROM into the sandboxed runner below — it executes entirely in your browser via WebAssembly; nothing is uploaded.
  3. Apply community .ips / .bps enhancement patches and verify them with the built-in CRC-32 checksum engine.
  4. Track your playthroughs and bookmarks in the grid below — dismiss this guide once you are comfortable.

Tip: Use a legally dumped copy of games you own — the dropzone runs locally with no server upload.

Tip: .ips / .bps patches are code-only and safe — the checksum engine verifies every applied byte.
